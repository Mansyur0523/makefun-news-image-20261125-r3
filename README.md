# 33 verified stories, 9 regions: US tariffs on 14 partners, Pakistan first astronaut, Hodeidah strikes

A one-page newspaper poster for Wednesday 25 November 2026: 33 verified
stories from 9 regions, drawn as a single 1024x1024 image. Round 3 is a
legibility redesign: fewer stories (33, down from 38) so every headline is
set large enough to read at 300px — leads at scale 3, features at scale 3,
briefs at scale 2, with no fine print anywhere.

## What is on the poster

- **Masthead:** WEDNESDAY 25 NOVEMBER 2026, with the count of stories and regions.
- **Number strip:** 14 partners hit by US tariffs; 20% top China tariff rate;
  4 tankers hit by mines; 30 killed in Niger State; 112 drones over Ukraine;
  S&P 500 close 7,182.78.
- **Region bar:** N AMER 8, ASIA 6, MID EAST 5, EUROPE 5, AFRICA 3,
  LAT AM 2, SPORT 1, CLIMATE 2, OCEANIA 1 (33 total).
- **3 leads:** US tariffs on 14 partners (China top rate 20%); Pakistan's
  first astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali reaches Tiangong on Shenzhou-24;
  Saudi-led coalition strikes Hodeidah port as Houthis threaten Jeddah.
- **10 features:** Dow claws back a 1,100-point drop; Cory Booker grills
  Nvidia over Jensen Huang's return; four tankers hit by mines in Hormuz
  (IRGC claim, not verified); OPEC blames the EU for the COP31 collapse;
  112 drones hit Odesa; Liverpool 3-0 and Barcelona 4-1 in the Champions
  League; 30 killed in Niger State; Africa debt can fuel growth ($93B);
  Cairo ring-road fire kills 21; judges urge dropping Netanyahu's Case 4000.
- **20 briefs:** US GDP revised to 4.3%, Microsoft code red, Fed hike seen
  in December, oil at $84, Embraer jet duty, Japan data-centre revolt,
  Takaichi meets the ICC chief, India okays a $1.2B plan, rupee at an
  8-month high, PhonePe eyes a $15B IPO, Merz warns on Russia, EU 50B euro
  quantum fund, Zaporizhzhia loses power, Equinor cuts output, Egypt-Cyprus
  Ptolemy drill, Egypt seeks 27 after a boat sinking, Haddad skips the
  Lula cabinet, Parana floods kill six, Singapore-Australia carbon deal,
  Australia digital duty law.
- **World map** with 13 numbered pins (leads 1-3, features 4-13) and a
  **DATE CHECK:** COP31 ended 22 Nov (the 25 Nov news is the blame game);
  the Odesa strike was 25 Nov while the 2 dead were 24 Nov; UCL scores
  shown are 25 Nov only.

## Sources

Reuters, AP, UEFA, OECD/African Union, Xinhua/CMSA reporting, 25 November
2026. Every figure on the poster was checked against same-day reporting
before drawing; claims that could not be verified independently (the IRGC
mine claims) are labelled as claims on the poster.

## How it is built

`entry.png` is generated by `gen.mjs` (kept outside the repo) with a
hand-built 5x7 bitmap font and a self-written PNG encoder — no vector
fonts, no image libraries. `main.ts` copies the image to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`) after validating the
PNG (magic, size, dimensions, chunk CRCs, IEND present).
